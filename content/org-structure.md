# WeBots / CHRC Org Structure

This chart reflects the proposed 2026-2027 operating structure.

- WeBots stays the umbrella student organization.
- Project JOSH and the WeBots CHRC Competition Team each have a Director of Engineering reporting directly to the WeBots President.
- CHRC is a separate competition organization led by the CHRC President, with VP Comp, two VP Logistics positions, VP Social Media, and VP Outreach.
- WeBots competitors receive only published CHRC rules, official clarifications, and the same information as other teams.
